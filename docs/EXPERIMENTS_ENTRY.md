
## YORAI — the coffee journey (2026-11-01)

Statuses: *built* (shipped, not validated with users), *dropped*, *idea*. The owner has not seen the site yet.

- **The page's colour temperature as the story.** Fog, sky, lights and the UI accent warm from mist blue-grey to crema gold as the coffee moves from tree to cup; no theme switch, no section boundaries. *built*. Hypothesis: a visitor can say "it got warmer" without being told, and reads the sections' dark register as the end of the journey rather than a new site. Measure: ask three people what changed as they scrolled.
- **Fog as the only cut.** Six environments on a line, the camera passing through exponential fog whose colour becomes the next chapter's — no crossfade, no black. *built*. Hypothesis: a mist crossing reads as travelling; a fade reads as a slideshow. Watch: the crossing lasts 28 % of a chapter (~60 vh) and may feel empty on a fast scroll; an *idea* is a few drifting particles in the crossing so the mist is never blank.
- **Transformation inside a chapter, not only between them.** Beans darken as the roast progresses; the stream starts and stops and the carafe fills during the pour; the grounds bloom. *built*. Hypothesis: a visitor who scrolls back and forth in the roast chapter notices the colour and stays longer. Measure: scroll reversals inside chapter IV (would need analytics the site does not have).
- **The near rows as leaf cards over blob bodies.** Only the shrubs beside the camera's aisle get 34 leaf cards each; the far rows stay smooth blobs in the fog. *built*. Hypothesis: detail where the camera is and fog everywhere else beats uniform detail at a third of the cost. Watch: the blob bodies read as rocks under backlight; *idea*: a darker, greener body material and a second card size.
- **Calm mode as a still-frame slideshow of the same scene.** One held pose per chapter with a short crossfade, rather than a separate set of images. *built*. Hypothesis: the held poses are as readable as the moving journey and cost nothing extra to maintain.
- **The reservation as an email, in the open.** The dialog composes the mailto and says so; "Copy summary" is the fallback. *built*. Hypothesis: for a small farm a human reply within a day beats a cart with no stock control. Measure: whether the owner asks for a checkout.
